Airbnb management in El Poblado

El Poblado is where many visitors to Medellín look first, so a short-term rental here competes with many other listings. What makes the difference is the listing and the price. We write and maintain your listing, lead with what guests in this part of the city search for (location, workspace, view, air conditioning) and adjust nightly rates with dynamic pricing based on season, events and local demand. If your apartment is within walking distance of places guests already know, such as Parque Lleras or Provenza, the listing says so clearly; if the street is lively at night, it says that too, because accurate listings lead to better reviews.

We keep your calendar synced across Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia and VRBO to avoid double bookings, coordinate check-ins and check-outs with your building’s reception, and schedule and supervise cleaning after every stay. Before launch we check two things with you: that your building’s reglamento de propiedad horizontal allows short stays, and that the apartment has an active Registro Nacional de Turismo (RNT). From then on we handle RNT, SIRE and TRA. See our Airbnb management in Medellín page for the full service.

Long-term rental management in El Poblado

Not every El Poblado apartment suits nightly guests. If your building restricts short stays, or you prefer fewer turnovers, we manage furnished stays of 30 nights or more for remote workers, professionals on assignment and families. Long stays mean fewer check-ins and cleanings and steadier income month to month, and our commission is lower: 12% + IVA on Basic or 15% + IVA on Premium. See long-term property management in Medellín for how short and long stays compare.

Building costs such as the cuota de administración, utilities and property tax keep running whether or not the apartment is booked. On the Premium plan we pay your property tax, HOA fees, utilities and internet for you and send a monthly accounting report, so nothing is missed while you are away.

Frequently asked questions

How much does property management cost in El Poblado?

With Medellín Concierge it is a commission on rental income, plus IVA: 17% (Basic) or 20% (Premium) for short stays, and 12% or 15% for stays of 30 nights or more. There are no upfront fees. See the full fee breakdown.

Can I rent my El Poblado apartment on Airbnb?

Only if your building’s reglamento de propiedad horizontal allows short stays and the apartment has an active Registro Nacional de Turismo (RNT), renewed every year (as of November 2026). We check the reglamento with you and handle RNT, SIRE and TRA; confirm your own legal and tax obligations with a qualified professional.

What if my building does not allow short stays?

We can manage the apartment for stays of 30 nights or more instead, on the long-stay rate of 12% (Basic) or 15% (Premium) + IVA. We review the reglamento with you before launch.

Where is your office?

In El Poblado, at Cl. 10 #42-28, Oficina 15, Medellín. We also manage properties in Laureles, Envigado, Sabaneta and across Medellín.

Can you manage my El Poblado apartment if I live abroad?

Yes. We handle guests, check-ins and check-outs, cleaning, maintenance, registrations and payments, and you follow everything in your owner portal. On Premium we also pay your property tax, HOA fees, utilities and internet. Repairs above a pre-agreed limit need your approval first.

When do I get paid?

Once a month, within the first 5 business days, for the previous month. Earnings are calculated using GAAP, net of our management fees and expenses.

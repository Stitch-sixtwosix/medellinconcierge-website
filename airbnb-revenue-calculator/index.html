How the calculator estimates your revenue

It uses three numbers and one simple formula:

  • Average nightly rate (USD): what guests pay per night on average across the month, not your peak-date price.
  • Occupancy: the share of nights that are booked. Nights booked per month = 30.4 × occupancy, so 70% is about 21 nights.
  • Gross monthly revenue = nightly rate × nights booked. The yearly figure is simply that month × 12.

The neighborhood and bedrooms buttons only set a starting nightly rate for the slider: USD 95 for a one-bedroom in El Poblado, 80 in Laureles, 70 in Envigado, 65 in Sabaneta and 60 elsewhere, plus USD 35 for each extra bedroom. They are a convenient starting point, not market data for your building. Move the rate and occupancy sliders to what comparable listings near you actually achieve, and the result updates instantly.

What moves the numbers

  • Nightly rate across the year. Demand changes with the season, local events and the day of the week. We adjust rates with dynamic pricing so peak dates are not underpriced and quiet weeks still fill.
  • Occupancy. A listing on Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia and VRBO reaches more guests than one platform alone; we keep all of them on one synced calendar so there are no double bookings.
  • Location and building. Area, view, amenities and how close the apartment is to transport and restaurants all shape what guests will pay. See our pages on El Poblado, Laureles, Envigado and Sabaneta.
  • The listing itself. Photos, description, minimum stays, reviews and fast replies to guests all affect both rate and occupancy.

What this estimate leaves out

  • Seasonality. The calculator uses one average month. Real months go up and down, and a new listing usually needs time to collect its first reviews.
  • Building rules. Some buildings’ reglamento de propiedad horizontal does not allow short stays. Check it before you count on Airbnb income; if short stays are not allowed, stays of 30 nights or more may be the option.
  • Registration. As of November 2026, an apartment rented to tourists for short stays in Colombia needs a Registro Nacional de Turismo (RNT), and foreign guests are reported to Migración Colombia through SIRE. We manage the RNT, SIRE and TRA for the properties we manage; read is Airbnb legal in Medellín? RNT, TRA and SIRE explained. Rules can change, so confirm your own obligations with a qualified professional.
  • Exchange rate. The calculator works in US dollars for convenience. Your income and costs in Colombia are in pesos, so the dollar figure moves with the exchange rate.

Revenue calculator: frequently asked questions

How much can an Airbnb apartment in Medellín earn?

It depends on the nightly rate the apartment can command and how many nights it is booked, which vary with the area, the building, the size and the season. The calculator on this page multiplies the two to give a monthly gross figure; for an estimate of your own property, request a free consultation.

How does the revenue calculator work?

Gross monthly revenue = average nightly rate × nights booked, where nights booked = 30.4 × occupancy. The neighborhood and bedroom buttons only set a starting nightly rate; adjust the rate and occupancy sliders to what comparable listings near you achieve.

Does the estimate include fees and taxes?

No. It is gross revenue, before platform fees, management fees, cleaning, utilities and taxes. Our commission for short stays is 17% + IVA on the Basic plan or 20% + IVA on Premium, which also pays your property taxes, HOA fees, utilities and internet.

What occupancy should I enter?

Use what comparable listings near your apartment achieve over a full year, not just in the busiest months. The slider starts at 70% as an example, not as a forecast for your property.

Can you give me an estimate for my specific property?

Yes. In a free consultation we look at your property, estimate its income and recommend a plan, for short stays, long stays of 30 nights or more, or both.

How much does Airbnb management cost in Medellín?

With Medellín Concierge, short-stay management costs 17% of rental income plus IVA on the Basic plan, or 20% plus IVA on Premium. Long stays of 30+ nights are 12% or 15% plus IVA. There are no upfront fees.

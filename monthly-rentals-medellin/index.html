Mid-term rentals for remote work, relocation and long visits

A mid-term rental sits between a few nights on Airbnb and a 12-month lease: a furnished apartment for one month or several, ready to live in from the day you arrive. It suits remote workers and digital nomads, people relocating to Medellín while they look for something permanent, work assignments, and families on a long visit. Each of our monthly listings shows the price per month and a one-time cleaning fee.

Why rent by the month directly with us

  • Current prices and availability. Our listings come live from HostFlow, the booking system every property we manage runs on.
  • A local team that knows each apartment. We manage these apartments ourselves, so we can tell you about the building, the street and what is included before you commit.
  • Answers on WhatsApp, in English or Spanish. Our team is based in El Poblado, and guests can reach us 24/7 during the stay.

Where to rent for a month in Medellín

Our monthly apartments are in El Poblado, Envigado, Medellín, Laureles, Ciudad del Río and Guayabal. El Poblado has the most restaurants, cafés and coworking spaces; Laureles is flat, residential and local; Envigado is quieter and good for everyday life. For a side-by-side comparison, read El Poblado vs Laureles vs Envigado for a month-long stay.

How to rent a furnished apartment by the month

  1. Choose an apartment from the list above, or tell us what you need: area, bedrooms and dates.
  2. Ask us on WhatsApp. We confirm availability for your dates and answer your questions about the apartment.
  3. Agree the stay in writing. Dates, price, what is included and the house rules.
  4. Move in. Our local team coordinates your check-in.

Before you sign: a monthly rental checklist

  • Know what the monthly price includes, for example utilities, internet and cleaning, and whether anything is charged on top.
  • Ask what you need to rent. Many Colombian landlords ask for a fiador (guarantor); our guide to renting a furnished apartment in Medellín without a fiador explains the alternatives.
  • Check how long you can stay in Colombia. See visitor stays and the digital nomad visa.
  • Get the agreement in writing, with the dates and what happens if you want to extend or leave early.

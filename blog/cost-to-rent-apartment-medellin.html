How Much Does It Cost to Rent an Apartment in Medellín in 2026?

24 November 2026 · 7 min read · Medellín Concierge

Short answer: The cost to rent an apartment in Medellín in 2026 depends mostly on three things: the neighborhood, whether it is furnished, and how long you stay. Public figures point to a rising market. Medellín's real estate observatory (OIME) put rents for apartments in El Poblado at COP 53,812 per square metre per month in July 2026, and the portal Ciencuadras put the average Medellín listing at about COP 4.9 million a month (El Colombiano, El Colombiano). Our own furnished monthly rentals start from COP 6.9 million per month as of November 2026 (monthly rentals page).

This guide is for travellers, remote workers and anyone planning a move to Medellín. It is general information as of November 2026, not legal or financial advice.

To turn the OIME figure into a monthly rent: a 90 m² apartment in El Poblado at COP 53,812 per m² works out to about COP 4.8 million a month, unfurnished and before utilities. That is a rough illustration from one data point, not a quote.

Why do the numbers vary so much?

You'll find guides online quoting very different figures for the same city. The usual reasons:

  • Asking price vs agreed price. Portal averages show what landlords ask, not what tenants end up paying.
  • Furnished vs unfurnished. Most public data is about unfurnished long-term leases. A furnished monthly apartment bundles furniture, equipment and often services, so it costs more on paper.
  • Neighborhood and estrato. El Poblado, Laureles and parts of Envigado sit at the top of the market. In Colombia, utilities are also billed by the property's socio-economic stratum (estrato), and, as of November 2026, homes in estratos 5 and 6 pay a contribution on top of the cost of the service (Ley 142 de 1994, art. 89).
  • Sample and date. A portal average changes with whatever is listed that week.

How much can rent go up in 2026?

For urban housing leases under Ley 820 de 2003, the landlord can raise the rent once every 12 months of the contract, by no more than the previous year's consumer price inflation (Ley 820 de 2003, art. 20). DANE put 2025 inflation at 5.10%, so as of November 2026 that is the cap for increases applied during 2026 (Noticias RCN). The increase is not automatic; it applies on the contract's anniversary.

Short furnished stays for tourists work differently: as of November 2026, apartments offered as vivienda turística must be registered in the National Tourism Registry, RNT (MinCIT), and the price is simply what your booking says. Which rules apply depends on how the contract is set up, so ask, and consult a qualified lawyer if anything is unclear.

How to budget for a Medellín apartment: a 5-step checklist

  1. Decide your length of stay. Under three months, compare furnished monthly prices; for a year or more, an unfurnished lease may be cheaper.
  2. Pick two or three neighborhoods, then compare prices within them, not across the city.
  3. Ask for the full monthly total in pesos: rent, building fee, utilities, internet and cleaning.
  4. Check the guarantee: fiador, insurance policy or deposit, and when you get it back.
  5. Get everything in writing before paying, and pay by a traceable method. If you're a foreign guest, bring your passport: as of November 2026, accommodation providers report foreign guests to Migración Colombia through SIRE (Migración Colombia).

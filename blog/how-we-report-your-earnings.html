How we report your earnings: GAAP statements and payouts in the first 5 business days

4 November 2026 · 3 min read · Medellín Concierge

Ask a few property managers in Medellín for a monthly financial statement and you will see the problem quickly. Some send a screenshot. Some send a WhatsApp message with a total. Some can’t send anything at all. If you can’t see what your property earned, you can’t know whether it is being managed well.

Statements prepared using GAAP

We prepare your earnings using GAAP, the Generally Accepted Accounting Principles. In practice, that means a standard, consistent set of accounting rules: income and expenses are recorded the same way every month, in the period they belong to, so one month’s statement can be compared with the next.

When you get paid

We pay out your earnings once a month. Within the first 5 business days of each month, you receive the previous month’s earnings, net of our management fees and expenses. Any additional cost, such as deep cleaning, minor repairs or restocking supplies, is discussed with you in advance, and repairs above a pre-agreed limit need your approval first.

Your owner portal

We are a professional property management company with our own software. Through your owner portal you can follow your property’s performance and your financial statements whenever you want, instead of waiting for someone to answer a message.

On Premium, your bills are in the statement too

Premium owners don’t pay their property’s bills themselves: we pay the property taxes, HOA fees, utilities and internet, and you receive a monthly accounting report that shows it all in one place.

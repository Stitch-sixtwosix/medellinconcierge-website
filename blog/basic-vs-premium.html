Basic or Premium: which management plan fits your property?

4 November 2026 · 4 min read · Medellín Concierge

Every owner we work with chooses between two plans. Both cover the day-to-day running of your rental, from the listing to the guests to the cleaning. The difference is how much you still want to handle yourself.

What both plans include

  • Development and optimization of your listing on Airbnb, with dynamic pricing to maximize revenue
  • Booking coordination and calendar synchronization, so you never get double bookings
  • Listing on Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia and VRBO
  • Full guest support, 24/7
  • Cleaning and maintenance, coordinated and supervised by our team
  • Legal compliance management, including RNT, SIRE and TRA
  • Emergency support

The rates

Both plans are a commission on your rental income, with no upfront fees. Long stays are stays of 30 nights or more.

Long stays (30+ nights)Short stays
Basic12% + IVA17% + IVA
Premium15% + IVA20% + IVA

What Premium adds

Premium is a completely hands-off experience. On top of everything in Basic, we pay your property’s bills for you: property taxes, HOA fees, utilities and internet. You also receive a monthly accounting report, so every payment is accounted for in one place.

Which one is right for you?

Choose Basic if you are happy to keep paying the property’s taxes, HOA fees and utility bills yourself, and you want us to run everything to do with guests, pricing, cleaning, maintenance and compliance.

Choose Premium if you live abroad, own more than one property, or simply never want to think about a Colombian utility bill again. You get your earnings and your statement each month, and nothing else to do.

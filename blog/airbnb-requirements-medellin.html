Airbnb Requirements in Medellín: Building Rules, the New POT and RNT (2026)

13 November 2026 · 7 min read · Medellín Concierge

Short answer: to rent an apartment on Airbnb in Medellín you need at least five things: land-use rules at your address that allow tourist homes, building bylaws (reglamento de propiedad horizontal) that expressly authorize it, an active National Tourism Registry (RNT), a written notice to the nearest police station, and a record of every guest (TRA, plus SIRE for foreigners). Miss any one of them and the city can suspend the rental, even if your RNT is up to date.

This guide, current as of November 2026, focuses on the two requirements that stop the most deals right now: building bylaws and the new POT (Medellín's land-use plan). For a full walkthrough of the RNT, TRA and SIRE, read Is Airbnb legal in Medellín? RNT, TRA and SIRE explained. This is general information, not legal or tax advice. We are not lawyers or tax advisors, so for your specific case consult a qualified professional and confirm with the relevant authority.

What are the requirements to rent on Airbnb in Medellín?

The Alcaldía de Medellín summarizes the rules in its tourist-home infographic (Spanish) and its Q&A What is allowed and what is not. Here is the full list, in the order we suggest checking it:

Items 4 to 6 come from Article 87 of Colombia's National Police and Coexistence Code (Law 1801 of 2016), according to the city's infographic. The Alcaldía also stresses a point many foreign owners miss: the RNT does not replace land-use rules. You need both.

What does Medellín's new POT say about short-term rentals?

Medellín's City Council approved the revised land-use plan (Plan de Ordenamiento Territorial, POT) in its second debate in the early hours of 5 November 2026, with 18 votes in favor, as reported by Minuto30. According to the Alcaldía's explainer Tourist homes and the POT: where yes, where no, the plan does not ban short-term rentals citywide. It sorts them by zone:

  • Low-mix zones (mainly residential): tourist homes are restricted.
  • Medium-mix zones: allowed only near transport corridors such as the Metro.
  • High-mix zones: allowed.

Outlets such as RCN Radio mention parts of El Poblado, Laureles and Belén among the areas where tourist homes could operate, but stress that not every property in those neighborhoods qualifies: the exact address matters, not the neighborhood name. Agencia PI reports that lodgings already operating with all requirements in place could continue.

Watch the dates: as of 13 November 2026, the sources we checked said the agreement was going to the mayor for signature and the final text was not yet public (ORP Urbanismo). Before you buy or furnish a unit for short-term rental, get the land-use status of the exact address and read the official text once it is published.

Does my building have to allow Airbnb in its bylaws?

Yes. Decree 1836 of 2021, which regulates tourist-home providers, requires that when a property is under the horizontal property regime (almost every apartment building and gated complex), the bylaws must expressly authorize tourist-home use. Bylaws that simply do not forbid it are not enough. The same decree requires building administrators to report units used as tourist homes to the Vice Ministry of Tourism.

How to check the bylaws in 4 steps

  1. Ask the building administration for the current reglamento, including every amendment.
  2. Find the section on the use of private units. The words that matter are vivienda turística, alojamiento turístico or arrendamiento por días. If it only says "residential use" or "family housing", there is no express authorization.
  3. Check the property's title certificate (certificado de tradición y libertad) to confirm any amendment was registered.
  4. Ask the administration whether other units are already reported as tourist homes and which house rules apply (visitor registration, common areas, move-ins).

What if the bylaws don't allow it?

The only route is to amend them. Under Article 46 of Law 675 of 2001, amending the bylaws requires a qualified majority of 70% of the ownership coefficients, counted over the whole building rather than only those present at the meeting, as Gerencie explains. The amendment is then recorded in a public deed and registered with the public registry office. In practice, reaching 70% in a building where most owners live on site can be hard. If you are buying for Airbnb income, buy where the authorization already exists; do not plan on changing it later.

What happens if you operate without meeting the requirements?

The Alcaldía describes the consequences of informal operation: suspension of the activity, fines and proceedings that can end in closure. The city reports that between January 2024 and 18 September 2026 it carried out 408 inspections and imposed 105 suspensions for breaches of Article 87. Separately, as we explain in our RNT guide, Law 2068 of 2020 provides for platforms to remove listings without an active RNT when the authorities ask.

Is the RNT about to change?

Possibly. In July 2026 MinCIT published a draft decree that would, among other changes, require a separate RNT for each tourist home. As of November 2026 we found no confirmation that it has been issued. Keep renewing your RNT between 1 January and 31 March, and check the decree's status before the 2027 renewal.

Checklist before you list

  • Land-use status of the address under the current POT.
  • Building bylaws with express, registered authorization.
  • RUT with DIAN.
  • Active RNT, with the number shown on Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia or VRBO.
  • Written notice to the nearest police station.
  • A process for TRA and SIRE on every booking.
  • A reminder to renew the RNT (1 January to 31 March).

FAQ

Can I rent on Airbnb in Medellín if the building bylaws say nothing about it?

No. Under Decree 1836 of 2021, buildings under horizontal property must expressly authorize tourist-home use in their bylaws. Silence is not authorization.

Does an RNT let me operate anywhere in Medellín?

No. The Alcaldía states that the RNT does not replace land-use rules. Under the new POT, approved by the City Council in November 2026 and not yet officially published when this guide was written, tourist homes are restricted in low-mix residential zones.

What vote does a building need to allow Airbnb?

Amending the bylaws requires 70% of the ownership coefficients under Article 46 of Law 675 of 2001, followed by a public deed and registration. For your specific case, consult a qualified professional.

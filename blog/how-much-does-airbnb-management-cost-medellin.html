How Much Does Airbnb Management Cost in Medellín? Fees, IVA and What's Included (2026)

17 November 2026 · 7 min read · Medellín Concierge

Short answer: there is no official or standard fee for managing an Airbnb in Medellín. Many managers charge a percentage of booking revenue, plus Colombia's VAT (IVA) if they are registered for it, and the owner separately pays the platform's commission and the property's own running costs. What you really pay comes down to three things: the percentage, what amount it is calculated on, and which services it covers.

This article shows you how to read a management proposal, what gets added on top of the percentage, and where to find our published fees. If you first want to know what a manager actually does day to day, read Airbnb management in Medellín: what a manager does, and for the big picture, our Medellín property management guide.

How do Airbnb managers in Medellín charge?

Three structures are common. Airbnb's own co-host tools let an owner pay a percentage of each booking, a fixed amount, the cleaning fee, or the cleaning fee plus a percentage, as the Airbnb Help Center on co-host payouts explains. A Bancolombia guide to short-term rentals (in Spanish) likewise notes that a manager can be paid either a percentage or a fixed cost.

The percentage model lines up incentives: if the property earns nothing, neither does the manager.

A percentage of what? The question that sets your real cost

Two proposals with the same percentage can cost you very different amounts. Before comparing, ask whether the percentage is taken from:

  1. The gross booking value (nightly rate plus cleaning fee).
  2. What Airbnb pays out to the host after its own commission.
  3. Net income after cleaning and other operating costs.

This illustrative example uses a booking subtotal of COP 1,000,000 and a hypothetical 10% rate (not a market rate and not ours; it only shows the effect of the base):

Do Colombian property managers charge VAT (IVA)?

As of November 2026, Colombia's general VAT rate is 19%, set by Article 468 of the Tax Statute (as amended by Law 1819 of 2016). In general, when the company providing management or brokerage services is registered for IVA, it adds that tax to its commission, as Gerencie.com explains for real estate agencies (in Spanish).

In practice: if a proposal says "X% + IVA", the effective cost of the commission is that percentage plus 19% of it. If it just says "X%", ask whether IVA is included. How IVA or withholding tax affects you personally depends on your tax situation, especially if you live abroad, so check with an accountant.

Paid separately: Airbnb's own commission

The platform's commission is not the manager's fee. Since 2025, Airbnb has moved most hosts, starting with those connected through property management software, to a single host-only fee. According to Hostfully, that fee is 15.5% of the booking subtotal (nightly rate, cleaning and extra guest or pet fees) in most countries. El Colombiano (in Spanish) reported on the change for hosts in Colombia. The exact rate for your listing appears in your Airbnb account settings, so confirm it there.

Booking.com, Expedia and VRBO charge their own commissions, which the owner also covers.

Other costs the owner usually pays

Even with a manager, the property's own costs stay with the owner:

  • Building administration fee (cuota de administración).
  • Utilities and internet.
  • Cleaning and laundry between stays (often recovered through the guest cleaning fee).
  • Consumables: toiletries, coffee, replacement linens.
  • Repairs and maintenance.
  • Liability insurance, and the yearly RNT renewal, which, according to MinCIT as of November 2026, is free and due between January 1 and March 31.
  • Taxes, such as property tax (predial) and income tax on rental earnings.

Always ask which of these the manager pays on your behalf, and how they are deducted and reported each month.

Checklist: 8 questions to ask before you sign

  • What is the percentage, and exactly what amount is it calculated on?
  • Is IVA included, or is it "+ IVA"?
  • Which services are included, and which are billed separately (cleaning, laundry, photos, repairs)?
  • Are there setup or exit fees?
  • Who pays the Airbnb and other platform commissions?
  • How often am I paid, and to which account (including a foreign account)?
  • What report do I get each month, and can I see bookings in real time?
  • Who keeps the RNT, SIRE and TRA up to date?

FAQ

How much does an Airbnb manager charge in Medellín?
There is no official or single rate. Many charge a percentage of bookings, plus IVA if they are registered for it. Always compare what the percentage is calculated on and what it includes. Our published plans are in the plans section.

Is Airbnb's commission included in the manager's fee?
Usually not. Airbnb's commission, 15.5% of the subtotal for most hosts on the host-only fee according to Hostfully, is deducted by the platform before payout. Confirm your rate in your account.

Do I pay VAT on property management in Colombia?
If the manager is registered for IVA, it charges 19% on its commission (Article 468, Tax Statute). How that affects you depends on your tax situation: consult an accountant.

El Poblado Apartments for Rent: Furnished Monthly Options and What's Included (2026)

22 November 2026 · 7 min read · Medellín Concierge

Short answer: El Poblado is the easiest part of Medellín to rent a furnished apartment by the month: it has the widest choice of furnished units, walkable cafés and coworking, and two Metro stations along its western edge. Before you book, check three things: which part of El Poblado the apartment is in (the area is large and hilly), exactly what the monthly price includes, and whether the contract fits your stay. This guide is for travellers, remote workers and people testing Medellín before a longer move. It is general information as of November 2026, not legal advice.

Where exactly is El Poblado?

El Poblado is Comuna 14 of Medellín, one of the city's 16 urban comunas according to the Alcaldía de Medellín. It is not one neighborhood but a whole district made up of many barrios, including Manila, Astorga, Castropol, Lalinde, La Florida, Los Balsos, El Tesoro, Las Lomas and La Aguacatala. Names you'll see in listings, such as "Provenza" or "Parque Lleras", are popular areas rather than official barrios.

That matters when you rent, because "El Poblado" on a listing can mean a flat street five minutes from the Metro or a hillside building where you'll take a taxi for every errand.

Which part of El Poblado should I rent in?

A simple way to think about it:

  • Lower Poblado, near the river and Avenida Las Vegas (for example around La Aguacatala, Manila and Astorga): flatter streets and the closest access to Metro Line A, which stops at Poblado and Aguacatala stations (Metro de Medellín).
  • Central Poblado, around Parque Lleras and Provenza: the most restaurants, bars and cafés within walking distance, and also the most noise on weekend nights.
  • Upper Poblado (barrios such as Los Balsos, Las Lomas, El Tesoro and Altos del Poblado): quieter, greener and more residential, but steep, so most people rely on taxis or ride apps.

How much does a furnished apartment in El Poblado cost per month?

There is no official average rent for furnished monthly apartments in El Poblado. Listing portals and booking sites show asking prices that change daily and mix furnished and unfurnished units, so treat them as a snapshot, not a market rate.

What drives the price in El Poblado is the same as elsewhere, only more pronounced: the exact barrio, the building's amenities, the size of the unit and the season. For our own apartments, the published figure is from COP 6.9 million per month as of November 2026; the exact price depends on the apartment, your dates and the length of stay, so email us for a quote. For a wider view of the city, see our guide to furnished apartments for rent in Medellín.

One local detail: in Colombia, utilities are billed by the property's socio-economic stratum (estrato), and homes in estratos 5 and 6 pay a contribution on top of the cost of the service that helps subsidise lower estratos (Ley 142 de 1994, art. 89, explained by the Constitutional Court, C-086 de 1998). Much of El Poblado's housing is in estratos 5 and 6, so ask which estrato an apartment is in. That's one reason an "all-in" monthly price can be better value than a lower rent plus bills.

How do I book an El Poblado apartment safely?

  1. Send your dates, budget and number of guests. It's the fastest way to get a real answer on availability.
  2. Confirm the exact barrio and building, not just "El Poblado".
  3. Get the price and the inclusions in writing, in pesos.
  4. See the apartment in person or on a live video call. Anyone can request a property's certificado de tradición y libertad from the Superintendencia de Notariado y Registro to check the registered owner.
  5. Read the contract before paying. For tourist stays, as of November 2026, apartments offered as vivienda turística must be registered in the National Tourism Registry (RNT) (MinCIT), so you can ask for the RNT number. Residential leases follow Ley 820 de 2003. Which applies depends on how the contract is set up; consult a qualified lawyer if anything is unclear.
  6. Bring your passport. Accommodation providers report foreign guests to Migración Colombia through the SIRE system (Migración Colombia).
  7. Pay by a traceable method and photograph the inventory at check-in.

For a first stay, yes for most people: it has the widest choice of furnished apartments and services within walking distance. If you want quiet nights or flat streets, choose lower or upper Poblado carefully, or compare Laureles and Envigado.

Are utilities included in El Poblado monthly rentals?

It depends on the apartment, so always ask. Many El Poblado homes are estrato 5 or 6, where utilities cost more, which makes an all-inclusive price worth comparing.

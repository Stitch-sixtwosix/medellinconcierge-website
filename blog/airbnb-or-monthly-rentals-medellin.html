Airbnb or Monthly Rentals: Which Suits Your Medellín Apartment? (2026)

25 November 2026 · 7 min read · Medellín Concierge

Short answer: if your building and zone allow tourist rentals and you (or your manager) can look after guests every day, Airbnb can earn more per night, in exchange for more work and more requirements. If not, or if you want less turnover, a furnished monthly rental (stays of one to six months) is the middle option: the apartment stays furnished and you keep some flexibility, but you deal with one tenant at a time and no cleaning every other day. A traditional one-year lease is still the most stable, lowest-effort choice.

Our post Airbnb vs long-term rental in Medellín compares the two extremes. This guide is about the middle ground, which many owners overlook.

General information as of November 2026. This is not legal or tax advice: we are not lawyers or tax advisors, so please consult a qualified professional about your situation.

What is a monthly rental, exactly?

It means renting your apartment furnished and fully equipped to one person or family for one or several months: professionals on relocation, remote workers, families who have just arrived and are looking for a permanent home, or Colombians back for a season. Each stay is longer than a tourist booking and shorter than a standard lease.

The difference from short-term rental isn't only price. It's length of stay, and length changes the rules (as of November 2026):

  • Tourist housing (vivienda turística): Decree 1836 of 2021 (in Spanish), from Colombia's Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Tourism, defines it as a home used for tourist accommodation for less than 30 days. It requires the National Tourism Registry (RNT) and, according to the Medellín mayor's office, express permission in the building's bylaws (reglamento de propiedad horizontal) and land use that allows it.
  • Urban housing lease: governed by Law 820 of 2003 (in Spanish). Article 5 says the term is whatever the parties agree and, if they don't set one, it is one year.

One practical catch: official summaries don't always use exactly the decree's wording ("less than 30 days"), so a stay of exactly 30 days can be a grey area. Before you set up a monthly contract, ask a lawyer and read your building's bylaws, which may have their own rules on how units are used.

Another one: as of November 2026, on Airbnb a "monthly stay" starts at 28 nights, and those bookings automatically get the long-term cancellation policy (Airbnb Help Center). So a 28- or 29-night booking is "monthly" to the platform but still under the decree's 30 days.

When does a monthly rental make sense?

Tick the ones that apply:

  • Your building's bylaws do not authorize tourist rentals, or the owners' assembly is discussing restricting them.
  • Your address is in a zone where land use limits tourist accommodation (the 2026 revision of Medellín's land-use plan, the POT, concentrates it in mixed-use zones; the mayor's office sums it up in Vivienda turística y POT: dónde sí, dónde no, in Spanish).
  • The apartment is already furnished and you don't want to sell or remove the furniture.
  • You want less wear and tear, less noise for neighbors and fewer cleanings than with Airbnb.
  • You want to use the apartment a few weeks a year, which is hard with a 12-month lease.
  • You live outside Medellín and would rather deal with one tenant at a time.

If you ticked three or more, it's worth running the numbers on monthly rentals before you decide.

Which earns more?

It depends on your building, neighborhood, unit size and season, and there is no official figure comparing the three models in Medellín. Be wary of citywide averages: run the exercise with your apartment's numbers.

  • Short-term: nightly rate × booked nights per year, minus platform fees, cleaning, utilities, internet, supplies, building fee, management fee and taxes.
  • Monthly: monthly rent × occupied months (count the empty weeks between tenants), minus utilities and internet if included, move-out cleaning, building fee, management fee and taxes.
  • Traditional: monthly rent × 12, minus empty months between leases, maintenance, management fee and taxes. For 2026, the maximum increase at renewal is 2025 inflation, which DANE put at 5.10% (Noticias RCN).

Always compare net, not gross, and count the time you or your manager will spend on each. For the full calculation, see how to calculate your Medellín rental yield. For taxes on that income, talk to an accountant.

What to check before renting by the month

  1. Your building's bylaws. See what they say about how units are used, move-ins, visitor registration and pets.
  2. A written contract with move-in and move-out dates, the rent, what's included (utilities, internet, cleaning) and the notice period.
  3. Guarantee. Law 820 does not allow cash deposits in urban housing leases (Art. 16); ask a lawyer which guarantees you can request given how your contract is set up.
  4. A photo inventory at move-in and move-out.
  5. Utilities in whose name. If they're included in the rent, put a reasonable cap in writing.
  6. Building fee paid up. The owner remains liable for it even when the unit is rented; we explain why in apartment management in Medellín.

FAQ

Do I need an RNT to rent my apartment by the month?

The RNT is required for tourist housing, which Decree 1836 of 2021 defines as accommodation for less than 30 days. A lease of several months is generally governed by Law 820 of 2003. Because there are nuances (stays of exactly 30 days, for example), ask a lawyer about your contract (information as of November 2026).

Can I rent by the month if my building doesn't allow Airbnb?

The express bylaw authorization the mayor's office requires refers to tourist housing. A monthly housing lease is a different arrangement, but read the full bylaws: they may have rules on how units are used. If in doubt, ask the building's administration and a lawyer.

Which earns more: Airbnb or monthly rentals?

There's no general answer and no official figure for Medellín. It depends on occupancy, rates, costs and the management time each model takes. Compare net yearly income using your own apartment's numbers; we're happy to do that exercise with you in a free consultation.

Six questions to ask before you hire a property manager in Medellín

4 November 2026 · 5 min read · Medellín Concierge

Handing your apartment to a manager is a big decision, especially if you live outside Colombia. Before you sign anything, ask these six questions. A professional manager will answer every one of them clearly. Here are our answers, so you know what to compare against.

1. Can you show me a sample monthly financial statement?

This is the single most revealing question. If a manager can’t put together a financial statement, they can’t show you what your property earned or where the money went. We prepare monthly statements using GAAP.

2. When exactly do I get paid?

“Monthly” is not an answer. Ask for the date. We pay the previous month’s earnings within the first 5 business days of each month.

3. Will I have my own access, or do I have to ask you for updates?

You should not have to chase anyone. We run our own software, and every owner gets an owner portal to follow their property’s performance and statements.

4. Who handles RNT, SIRE and TRA?

Short-term tourist rentals in Colombia need the National Tourism Registry (RNT), migration reporting for foreign guests (SIRE) and the Tourism Registration Card (TRA). Ask who files them and who keeps them up to date. With us, both plans include legal compliance management.

5. What does the fee include, and what costs extra?

Get the percentage, whether it includes IVA, and a list of what is charged on top. Our plans are 12% to 20% plus IVA depending on the plan and length of stay, with no upfront fees. Extra costs such as deep cleaning or minor repairs are discussed with you in advance.

6. What happens if I want to stop?

A good manager makes leaving easy. You can pause or end our services with advance notice, as set out in our agreement, and we provide a smooth handover if you decide to self-manage or switch providers.

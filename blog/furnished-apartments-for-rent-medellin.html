Furnished Apartments for Rent in Medellín: Prices, Neighborhoods and How to Book (2026)

20 November 2026 · 7 min read · Medellín Concierge

Short answer: A furnished apartment in Medellín rented by the month is the simplest way to stay a few weeks or months without a Colombian co-signer or a one-year lease. The price depends mainly on the neighborhood, the size of the unit and what the rent includes; at Medellín Concierge, our monthly furnished rentals start from COP 6.9 million per month (as of November 2026, see our monthly rentals page). To book, send the owner or manager your dates, budget and number of guests, confirm what is included in writing, and pay only after you have verified the apartment and signed a contract.

This guide explains what drives the price, which neighborhoods renters usually compare, and the exact steps to book safely. It is general information as of November 2026, not legal advice.

How much does a furnished apartment in Medellín cost per month?

There is no official average price for furnished monthly rentals in Medellín. Listing portals such as Fincaraíz and Metrocuadrado show asking prices that change every day and mix furnished and unfurnished units, so treat any number you see there as a snapshot, not a market rate.

For our own apartments, the figure we publish is simple: monthly furnished rentals from COP 6.9 million per month, as of November 2026. The exact price for your stay depends on the apartment, the dates and the length of stay, so email us for a quote.

Prices in Colombia are quoted in pesos. If you are budgeting in dollars or euros, use the day's official exchange rate (TRM), which is certified by the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia, and remember that your bank or card will apply its own rate and fees.

Which neighborhoods should I compare?

Most people looking for a furnished apartment for a month or more end up comparing three areas. We covered them in detail in El Poblado vs Laureles vs Envigado: where to stay in Medellín for a month; here is the short version:

  • El Poblado (Comuna 14 of Medellín, according to the Alcaldía de Medellín): the most restaurants, cafés, coworking spaces and services in English. Convenient for a first stay; busier and noisier near the nightlife streets.
  • Laureles (part of Comuna 11, Laureles-Estadio): flatter, more residential and with a local feel, still well connected to the rest of the city.
  • Envigado: a separate municipality just south of Medellín in the Valle de Aburrá metropolitan area, quieter and more family-oriented.

How do I book a furnished apartment in Medellín?

Here is the process we recommend, whoever you rent from:

  1. Send your dates, budget and number of guests. This is the fastest way to get a real answer about availability and price. Mention if you need parking, a pet-friendly building or a workspace.
  2. Ask what the price includes. Utilities, internet, the building fee, cleaning and any deposit or guarantee should be written down, not just mentioned on a call.
  3. Verify the apartment and the person renting it. See it in person or on a live video call. Anyone can request a property's certificado de tradición y libertad from the Superintendencia de Notariado y Registro to check the registered owner.
  4. Read the contract before paying. It should state the dates, the monthly price, what is included, house rules and what happens if you leave early.
  5. Have your passport ready. As of November 2026, accommodation providers in Colombia report foreign guests to Migración Colombia through the SIRE system, and the reporting rules ask for a valid passport or another document authorised by Migración Colombia (Resolución 2357 de 2020, Migración Colombia).
  6. Pay by a traceable method and keep every receipt.
  7. Check in and document the inventory. Take photos of the furniture and condition on arrival, so the check-out is simple.

Short stay or monthly contract: does it matter?

It can. In Colombia, as of November 2026, apartments offered to tourists as vivienda turística must be registered in the National Tourism Registry (RNT), which is managed for the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Tourism through the chambers of commerce (MinCIT, RNT portal). For short stays, it is reasonable to ask for the listing's RNT number.

As of November 2026, longer residential leases are governed by Ley 820 de 2003, which, among other things, limits cash deposits for urban housing leases (Article 16). Whether a specific furnished stay is a tourist stay or a residential lease depends on how the contract is set up, so read it carefully and consult a qualified lawyer if anything is unclear. Booking checklist

  • Dates, budget and number of guests sent
  • Monthly price confirmed in pesos, in writing
  • What's included listed: utilities, internet, building fee, cleaning
  • Apartment seen in person or on live video
  • Owner or manager identity checked
  • Written contract read before paying
  • Passport ready for check-in
  • Payment made by a traceable method, receipts saved

How to Set Up an Apartment for Airbnb in Medellín: Furnishing, Photos and Listing Checklist (2026)

16 November 2026 · 7 min read · Medellín Concierge

Short answer: setting up an Airbnb in Medellín happens in two layers. First the paperwork: the address and the building bylaws must allow tourist rentals, and you need an active National Tourism Registry (RNT) number, because Airbnb asks for it before a Colombian listing can be published. Then the product: a fully furnished unit with the essentials guests filter for (fast wifi, a real kitchen, a workspace), good photos and a complete, honest listing. Do the paperwork first. A beautiful apartment that can't legally operate is an expensive mistake.

This checklist is current as of November 2026 and is general information, not legal or tax advice. Rules change, so verify each point with the relevant authority or a qualified professional before you spend money.

What do you need before you furnish anything?

Check these before you buy a single sofa. Each one can stop a short-term rental on its own:

  1. Land use at your address. Medellín's new land-use plan (POT) sorts tourist homes by zone, so the exact address matters more than the neighborhood name. We explain it in Airbnb requirements in Medellín.
  2. Building bylaws. Under Decree 1836 of 2021, if the building is under the horizontal property regime, its bylaws (reglamento de propiedad horizontal) must expressly authorize tourist-home use.
  3. RNT. Registration is done online through Confecámaras and renewed every year. When the requirement took effect in 2022, La República reported that hosts must enter their RNT number to complete a listing on Airbnb in Colombia.
  4. Insurance. The Alcaldía de Medellín's tourist-home infographic lists a third-party liability policy (póliza de responsabilidad civil extracontractual) as a requirement, citing Article 32 of Law 2068 of 2020.
  5. Operating duties. The same infographic lists a written notice to the nearest police station, guest registration cards (TRA) and reporting of foreign guests to Migración Colombia (SIRE). Our guide Is Airbnb legal in Medellín? RNT, TRA and SIRE explained walks through each one.

Coming changes: as of November 2026, a draft decree from MinCIT proposes, among other things, that every listing display its RNT number visibly and that platforms check it periodically (Vanguardia). It is a draft, not law, but it is a good reason to keep your registrations clean from day one.

How should you furnish an apartment for Airbnb in Medellín?

Furnish for the guest who will actually book, not for yourself. In Medellín that is often a mix of tourists, business travellers and remote workers staying weeks at a time, so durability and function matter more than decoration.

Airbnb's own guide to the amenities guests want names wifi, a kitchen, free parking, a pool and air conditioning among the amenities guests search for most, and advises hosts to list every amenity they offer, because many guests use search filters. You can't add a pool, but you can control the rest.

Why do photos and the listing text matter so much?

Guests decide from the photos and the first lines of the listing. A few principles that apply to every unit:

  • Shoot in daylight with lights on, the bed made and surfaces clear.
  • Show every room and the details people filter for: the desk, the kitchen, the washing machine, the view, the building entrance.
  • Be accurate. Wide-angle lenses that make a small room look large lead to disappointed guests and poor reviews.
  • Write the description for the guest's questions: how far it is to the Metro or the nearest supermarket, whether there is a lift, the internet speed you measured, and the house rules from the building bylaws.
  • Complete the amenities list so the listing appears when guests filter, as Airbnb itself recommends.
  • Publish a house manual with wifi details, how the appliances work, check-out steps and building rules.

What does it cost to launch an Airbnb in Medellín?

It depends too much on the unit's size, condition and finish level for a single honest figure, and we won't invent one. Build your own budget from real quotes: furniture and appliances, linens and kitchenware (with spares), photography, insurance, and the months of cash flow before bookings stabilize. Then check whether the numbers still work with our guide to Medellín rental yield, which shows how to go from gross to net return. If the result is weak, a long-term rental may suit the property better.

FAQ

Can I list my apartment on Airbnb in Colombia without an RNT?
No. The RNT is required to provide tourist lodging, and Airbnb asks for the number during listing setup in Colombia, as La República reported. You also need land-use rules and building bylaws that allow tourist rentals.

Does an Airbnb in Medellín have to be fully furnished?
For short stays, guests expect a ready-to-live unit: furniture, a working kitchen, linens, towels and wifi. Airbnb's guide to the amenities guests want shows that guests filter by features such as wifi and a kitchen, so missing basics can keep your listing out of their search results.

Do I need insurance to rent on Airbnb in Medellín?
According to the Alcaldía de Medellín's infographic, tourist homes need a third-party liability policy (Law 2068 of 2020, Article 32). Ask an insurer about coverage, and consult a qualified professional for your specific case.

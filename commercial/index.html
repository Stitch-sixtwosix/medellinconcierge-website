Commercial hospitality management

Hotels, full buildings and community hospitality operations in Medellín, run for their owners by the same team and systems that manage our apartments.

Who we work with

Hotels and boutique hotels

Independent hotels that want stronger online distribution, smarter pricing and dependable guest service, without giving up their own identity.

Full buildings

Aparthotels and buildings of furnished units run as one operation: every unit priced, cleaned, maintained and reported together.

Community hospitality

Coliving, residential communities and buildings that mix short and long stays, where guests and residents share the same spaces.

What we run for you

One operator for the whole property, with one point of contact for owners and investors.

  • Distribution on Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia and VRBO, with synced calendars and dynamic pricing
  • 24/7 guest support: reservations, check-ins and guest communication
  • Housekeeping and maintenance, coordinated and supervised
  • Common areas, house rules and resident relations in shared buildings
  • RNT, SIRE and TRA registrations and reporting
  • Owner portal with monthly GAAP financial statements

How we take over an operation

  1. Operational review

    We visit the property and review how it runs today: occupancy, rates, staffing, costs and registrations. Then we tell you where we see room to improve.

  2. Proposal

    You receive a management proposal with the scope, the transition plan and a fee structure that fits the size of the operation.

  3. Transition and operation

    We set up registrations, listings and systems, then run day-to-day operations and report to you every month.

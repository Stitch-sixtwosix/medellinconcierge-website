What rental property management is (and what it is not)

Rental property management is the day-to-day work of turning a property into income for its owner. It is not the same as two services owners often confuse it with:

  • Building administration (propiedad horizontal). Every building or gated complex has its own administration that the co-owners appoint to run the common areas and collect the HOA fee (cuota de administración). That is a different service. As your manager, we deal with your building’s administration on your behalf: rules, access for guests and repairs.
  • A traditional rental agency (inmobiliaria). An agency typically finds one tenant for a long residential lease, often for an unfurnished apartment at a fixed rent. We manage furnished apartments and houses rented by the night or by the month and look after every guest, while you receive one payout and one statement a month. The difference is explained in more detail on our long-term property management page.

What our rental property management includes

  • Setup and listing: we help prepare the apartment, take the photos and publish it on Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia and VRBO.
  • Pricing: nightly or monthly rates with dynamic pricing, so the calendar fills without underpricing your property.
  • Guests: messages 24/7, check-ins and check-outs coordinated with your building, and cleaning between stays.
  • Maintenance: repairs with local providers; anything above a limit we agree with you in advance needs your approval first.
  • Registrations: we manage the RNT, SIRE and TRA that short stays need.
  • Payouts and reports: the previous month’s earnings within the first 5 business days, net of our fees and expenses, with statements calculated using GAAP in your owner portal.
  • Bills (Premium plan): we also pay your property tax, HOA fees, utilities and internet and send you a monthly accounting report.

Short stays or monthly stays: which suits your property?

Short stays (under 30 nights, on Airbnb and similar platforms) cost 17% + IVA on Basic or 20% + IVA on Premium. They need a building whose reglamento de propiedad horizontal allows them and an active RNT, and they usually earn more in peak season with more variation month to month. Monthly stays (30 nights or more) cost 12% + IVA on Basic or 15% + IVA on Premium. There are fewer check-ins and cleanings and income is steadier, and they can be an option where a building restricts short stays. See long-term property management in Medellín. Registrations and compliance

Short stays in Colombia need an active Registro Nacional de Turismo (RNT), renewed every year, foreign guests are reported to Migración Colombia through SIRE, and we also handle the TRA. For long-term leases, Medellín has a landlord registration (matrícula de arrendador; Medellín city hall, in Spanish). This is how things stand as of November 2026. We manage registrations, not tax or legal advice: rental income in Colombia can carry tax obligations, so confirm yours with a qualified professional.

Frequently asked questions

How much does rental property management cost in Medellín?

With Medellín Concierge: 12% of rental income + IVA for stays of 30 nights or more and 17% + IVA for short stays on the Basic plan, or 15% and 20% + IVA on Premium, which also pays your property tax, HOA fees, utilities and internet. There are no upfront fees. See the full fee breakdown.

Is rental property management the same as building administration?

No. Your building or complex has its own administration, appointed by the co-owners, that runs the common areas and collects the HOA fee. Rental property management looks after your own unit as a rental: guests, cleaning, maintenance, registrations and your payout. As your manager we deal with the building’s administration for you.

Do you manage houses or only apartments?

Both. We manage furnished apartments and houses for short stays and for stays of 30 nights or more in El Poblado, Laureles, Envigado, Sabaneta and across Medellín.

Does my property need to be furnished?

Yes, we manage furnished rentals. If your property is not furnished yet, our setup checklist goes room by room, and in a free consultation we tell you what it needs.

Can you manage my property if I live outside Colombia?

Yes. We handle guests, check-ins, cleaning, maintenance, the building administration and RNT, SIRE and TRA on the ground, and you follow everything in your owner portal. Repairs above a limit we agree in advance need your approval first.

When do I get paid?

Once a month, within the first 5 business days, for the previous month. Earnings are calculated using GAAP, net of our management fees and expenses.

What Sabaneta is like to stay in

Sabaneta is a separate municipality in the south of the Valle de Aburrá, next to Envigado, and is often described as the smallest municipality in Colombia by area (Blu Radio). It is compact and residential, with its own main park and local life, and quieter than El Poblado.

Getting around: Sabaneta station on Metro Line A sits between Itagüí and La Estrella, so you ride north to Poblado station and the rest of Medellín without changing lines (Sabaneta station; Metro de Medellín, Line A). Ask us how far an apartment is from the station and from the main park.

Sabaneta, Envigado or El Poblado: which suits your stay?

  • Choose Sabaneta if you want a calm, residential base with a direct Metro ride into Medellín: families, remote workers and people visiting relatives or getting to know the south of the valley. Because it lies south of Envigado, the trip to El Poblado and the city centre is longer.

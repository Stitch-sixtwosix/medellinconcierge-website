Do you need air conditioning in Medellín?

It depends on you more than on the apartment. Ask for it if you sleep better in a cool room, if you will work from the apartment in the afternoons, or if you are staying a month or more and want the choice. If you only want it for sleeping, tell us: what matters then is a unit in the bedroom. The other 14 homes we list do not have air conditioning; they are on our main rentals page. Also bringing a car? See our apartments with parking.

What to ask before you book

  • Which rooms have a unit: every bedroom, the living room, or only the main bedroom? Send us the reference and we tell you before you book.
  • For a monthly stay, what the price includes: ask whether electricity and other utilities are included or billed apart for the apartment you like, and get the answer in writing.
  • Other features you need: each listing also shows “Washer”, “Elevator” or “Parking” when the apartment has them.

How to rent an apartment with air conditioning direct

  1. Browse the listings. Photos, prices and availability come live from HostFlow, the platform every property we manage runs on, so what you see is current.
  2. Email us or tap “I’m interested”. Send your dates and number of guests to sales@medellinconcierge.net, or tap “I’m interested” on a listing to write on WhatsApp. Either way we answer your questions about the apartment, the building and the area.
  3. Confirm your stay. We agree your dates and the details of the stay with you. Our local team, based in El Poblado, coordinates your check-in, and guests can reach us 24/7 during the stay.

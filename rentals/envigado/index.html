What Envigado is like to live in

Envigado is a separate municipality just south of Medellín and part of the Valle de Aburrá metropolitan area (La FM); in practice the two cities run into each other. It has its own centre, main park, markets and services, and many parts of it feel like a small town inside the metropolitan area. It is generally less touristic than El Poblado, which is exactly why many people who come to live and work for a while choose it.

Getting around: Metro Line A runs south through Ayurá and Envigado stations, so you reach Poblado station in a few stops without changing lines (Metro de Medellín, Line A). Parts of Envigado are flat and walkable and others climb the hillside, where most people use taxis or ride apps, so ask us how far an apartment is from the station and from the shops you will use every day.

Envigado or El Poblado: which suits your stay?

  • Choose Envigado if you want quiet nights, room to work from home, local restaurants and markets, and everyday life more than nightlife: remote workers, families and people trying the area before a move.
  • Choose El Poblado if you are new to Medellín, want to walk to cafés, coworking spaces and restaurants, and go out often. See our furnished apartments for rent in El Poblado.

Because Envigado lies south of Medellín, the trip to the city’s main sights is longer. Our guide El Poblado vs Laureles vs Envigado for a month-long stay compares the three areas in detail.

Before you book in Envigado: a quick checklist

  • Ask where exactly the property is. Whether it is in a flat part or up the hill, and how far it is from the Metro, supermarkets and the main park.
  • Know the total price. Ask what it includes (for example utilities, internet and cleaning) and whether anything is charged on top.
  • For short stays, check the RNT. Ask for the listing’s RNT number and look it up on the public RNT system.
  • Get the dates and rules in writing, including check-in and check-out times and the building’s rules.

Is Envigado part of Medellín?

No. Envigado is a separate municipality just south of Medellín, part of the Valle de Aburrá metropolitan area, and the two cities run into each other. Metro Line A connects it with El Poblado and the rest of Medellín.

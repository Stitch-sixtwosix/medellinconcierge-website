What El Poblado is like to live in

El Poblado is Comuna 14 of Medellín, one of the 16 comunas of the city’s urban area (Alcaldía de Medellín). It sits on the hillside in the southeast of the city and has the highest concentration of hotels, restaurants, coworking spaces and shopping malls; areas such as Parque Lleras and Provenza are known for their nightlife. For a first stay in Medellín that means almost everything is close, and many businesses are used to serving foreigners.

Getting around: Metro Line A has stations at Poblado and Aguacatala along the river at the bottom of the hill (El Colombiano). Higher up the hill, most people use taxis or ride apps, so ask us how far an apartment is from the places you will go every day.

Is El Poblado right for your stay?

  • Good fit if you want to walk to cafés, gyms and restaurants, are new to Medellín and want services in English nearby, or enjoy going out in the evening.
  • Think twice if you are sensitive to noise (especially near the nightlife streets on weekends), want flat streets for walking or cycling (much of El Poblado is steep), or are on a tight budget: guides consistently describe it as the most expensive area of the city (Expat Exchange).

Prefer somewhere quieter or flatter? See our rentals in Laureles, Envigado and Sabaneta, or compare the areas in El Poblado vs Laureles vs Envigado for a month-long stay.

Before you book in El Poblado: a quick checklist

  • Ask where exactly the apartment is. How steep the walk is, and how far it is from the Metro and from the nightlife streets if you want quiet nights.
  • Know the total price. Ask what it includes (for example utilities, internet and cleaning) and whether anything is charged on top.
  • For short stays, check the RNT. Ask for the listing’s RNT number and look it up on the public RNT system.
  • Get the dates and rules in writing, including check-in and check-out times and the building’s rules.

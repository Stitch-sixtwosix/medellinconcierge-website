Is a 2-bedroom the right size?

Our 2-bedroom apartments sleep 3 to 6 guests and have 2 or 3 bathrooms. They suit a couple who needs a room to work in, two friends sharing, or a small family. If you are one or two people and do not need the second room, see our 1-bedroom apartments and studios. For larger groups, see our apartments with 3 or more bedrooms.

How to rent a 2-bedroom apartment direct

  1. Browse the listings. Photos, prices and availability come live from HostFlow, the platform every property we manage runs on, so what you see is current.
  2. Email us or tap “I’m interested”. Send your dates and number of guests to sales@medellinconcierge.net, or tap “I’m interested” on a listing to write on WhatsApp. Either way we answer your questions about the apartment, the building and the area.
  3. Confirm your stay. We agree your dates and the details of the stay with you. Our local team, based in El Poblado, coordinates your check-in, and guests can reach us 24/7 during the stay.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a 2-bedroom apartment for rent in Medellín?

As of 23 November 2026, our furnished 2-bedroom apartments cost from $6.900.000 to $15.000.000 COP a month for stays of 30 nights or more and from $200.000 COP a night for shorter stays, plus a one-time cleaning fee. Prices are set in Colombian pesos.

Are the 2-bedroom apartments furnished?

Yes. Every apartment on this page is furnished and ready to move into.

Can I rent a 2-bedroom apartment in Medellín for one month?

Yes. The apartments listed by the month take stays of 30 nights or more. For a shorter stay, choose one listed by the night.

Which neighborhoods have 2-bedroom apartments?

As of 23 November 2026, we have 2-bedroom apartments in El Poblado (7), Envigado (1), Sabaneta (2) and other parts of Medellín (4).

Do I rent from the owner or from an agency?

You rent direct from Medellín Concierge, the company that manages the apartment for its owner. Each apartment belongs to a private owner who has hired us to manage it, and you book directly with our local team instead of through a booking platform. We confirm the price, your dates and what is included in writing.

Can I ask by email instead of WhatsApp?

Yes. Write to sales@medellinconcierge.net with your dates, the number of guests and the area you prefer.

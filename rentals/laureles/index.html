What Laureles is like to live in

Laureles-Estadio lies west of the Medellín River. It is mostly flat, with tree-lined streets, circular avenues and lower buildings than El Poblado, and expat guides often recommend it for its residential, local feel, with plenty of cafés, restaurants and parks (Medellin Guru). Because it is flat, many daily errands can be done on foot.

Getting around: Metro Line B serves the area, and Estadio station is close to the Unicentro and Obelisco shopping centres (Metro de Medellín). Ask us how far an apartment is from the station and from the supermarket you would use every day.

Laureles or El Poblado: which suits your stay?

  • Choose Laureles if you want a residential neighborhood with a local feel, flat streets to walk and a calm place to work for a month or more: remote workers, slow travellers and people trying the city before a move.
  • Choose El Poblado if you are new to Medellín and want the widest choice of restaurants, coworking spaces and nightlife close by. See our furnished apartments for rent in El Poblado.

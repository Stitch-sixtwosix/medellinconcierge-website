Do you need parking in Medellín?

Only if you will have a car or motorcycle. Many people staying in El Poblado or Laureles get around on foot, by taxi or ride-hailing app, or on the Metro, and do not need a space. If you are driving in from another city or renting a car for trips out of Medellín, choose one of these. The other 23 homes we list do not have parking; they are on our main rentals page. Need air conditioning too? See our apartments with air conditioning.

What to ask about the parking space

  • Where it is: inside the building, and covered or not?
  • How many vehicles, and what size: does it fit an SUV, or a motorcycle as well as a car?
  • Whether it is included in the price shown. The listings only say the apartment has parking, so send us the reference and we confirm all of this in writing before you book.

How to rent an apartment with parking direct

  1. Browse the listings. Photos, prices and availability come live from HostFlow, the platform every property we manage runs on, so what you see is current.
  2. Email us or tap “I’m interested”. Send your dates and number of guests to sales@medellinconcierge.net, or tap “I’m interested” on a listing to write on WhatsApp. Either way we answer your questions about the apartment, the building and the area.
  3. Confirm your stay. We agree your dates and the details of the stay with you. Our local team, based in El Poblado, coordinates your check-in, and guests can reach us 24/7 during the stay.

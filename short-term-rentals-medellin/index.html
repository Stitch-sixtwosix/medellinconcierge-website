Short-term rentals in Medellín, booked direct

A short-term rental is a furnished apartment you rent by the night, ready to live in from the day you arrive: a kitchen, your own space and a local team a message away. It suits a holiday, a business trip, a family visit, or a first week in the city while you look for a monthly rental. Each listing on this page shows a “from” price per night and a one-time cleaning fee; as of 22 November 2026, cleaning fees on our short-term rentals run from $80.000 to $230.000 COP. The price for your exact dates comes in our written quote.

How to book a short-term rental with us

  1. Choose an apartment from the list above, or tell us what you need: area, bedrooms, number of guests and dates.
  2. Email us or ask on WhatsApp. Write to sales@medellinconcierge.net or tap “Ask about” on a listing. We answer in English or Spanish.
  3. Get your quote in writing. We confirm availability, the total price for your dates including the cleaning fee, and the house rules.
  4. Arrive. Our local team, based in El Poblado, coordinates your check-in, and guests can reach us 24/7 during the stay.

Short stays in Colombia: RNT and SIRE

Renting an apartment by the night is a tourist rental. In Colombia it needs a Registro Nacional de Turismo (RNT), and foreign guests are reported to Migración Colombia through SIRE; as the property manager, we handle both for the apartments we manage (as of November 2026). Before you book any short-term rental in Medellín, ask for the listing’s RNT number and look it up on the public RNT system. Rules for tourist rentals can change; this reflects how things stand as of November 2026.

Short stay or monthly rental?

If you are staying 30 nights or more, our monthly rentals in Medellín are priced by the month instead of by the night. Not sure where to stay? El Poblado has the most restaurants, cafés and nightlife; Envigado and Sabaneta, just south of Medellín, are quieter and good for everyday life. Read El Poblado vs Laureles vs Envigado to compare.

Before you book: a short-stay checklist

  • Check the RNT on the public RNT system.
  • Know the total price, including the cleaning fee and anything charged on top.
  • Get the dates and rules in writing, including check-in and check-out times and the building’s rules.
  • Know who to call. Make sure someone answers 24/7 if something goes wrong during your stay.

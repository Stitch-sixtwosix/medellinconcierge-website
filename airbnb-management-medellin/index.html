Airbnb management in Medellín

Medellín Concierge is a local Airbnb management company based in El Poblado. We run short-term rentals in Medellín, Envigado and Sabaneta for owners who live here and for owners who live abroad: the listing, the pricing, the guests, the cleaning and the paperwork. You keep ownership and full visibility; we handle the day-to-day so your apartment earns without becoming a second job.

Full-service Airbnb management

Full-service means one team owns every step. We prepare the apartment for guests, set up or take over your listings on Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia and VRBO, and keep the calendars synced so you avoid double bookings. Before launch we check the legal side: your building's reglamento de propiedad horizontal has to allow short stays, and the property needs an active Registro Nacional de Turismo (RNT). We handle RNT, SIRE and TRA registrations and guest reporting, and pay out the previous month's earnings within the first 5 business days, with GAAP statements in your owner portal.

Listing optimization and dynamic pricing

Pricing matters as much as demand. We write and maintain your listing, order the photos around what guests look for (location, workspace, view, air conditioning) and adjust nightly rates with dynamic pricing based on season, events and local demand. Minimum stays and weekly or monthly discounts are tuned so the calendar fills without underpricing peak dates. Want a starting point? Our revenue calculator estimates what your property could earn on Airbnb.

Guest communication, cleaning and check-ins

Guests get answers 24/7, before, during and after their stay. We coordinate check-ins and check-outs with your building, schedule and supervise cleaning after every stay, and coordinate maintenance with local providers. Repairs above a limit we agree with you in advance are approved by you first. Reviews are answered promptly, because ratings matter to every future guest.

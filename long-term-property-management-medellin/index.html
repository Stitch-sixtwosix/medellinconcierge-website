Management for stays of 30 nights or more

A long stay is any booking of 30 nights or more. Medellín attracts many people who want a furnished home for a month or longer, such as remote workers, professionals on assignment and families getting to know the city, and they look for apartments on the same platforms as short-stay guests. We list your apartment on Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia and VRBO, set monthly rates with dynamic pricing and tune monthly discounts so the calendar fills without underpricing your apartment.

From there we handle the day-to-day: guest questions 24/7, check-ins and check-outs with your building, cleaning between stays and maintenance with local providers. Monthly stays can also be an option when your building’s reglamento de propiedad horizontal restricts short stays; we review the reglamento with you before launch.

Payments, maintenance and compliance

We pay out once a month, within the first 5 business days, for the previous month. Earnings are calculated using GAAP, net of our management fees and expenses, and you can see bookings, statements and payouts in your owner portal at any time. Repairs above a limit we agree with you in advance are approved by you first. On the Premium plan we also pay your property tax, HOA fees, utilities and internet and send you a monthly accounting report, which helps most when you live abroad (see property management in Colombia for foreign owners).

On compliance: if your apartment also takes short stays, it needs an active Registro Nacional de Turismo (RNT), renewed every year, and we manage RNT, SIRE and TRA for you. Whether an apartment rented only for stays of 30 nights or more needs one depends on how it is rented, so check your case. This is how things stand as of November 2026. We manage registrations, not tax or legal advice: rental income in Colombia can carry tax obligations, especially for non-resident owners, so confirm yours with a qualified professional.

Why owners choose long stays

Long stays trade some peak-night income for predictability. There are fewer turnovers, so cleaning costs and wear on the apartment go down, and you think less about occupancy. Our commission is lower too: 12% + IVA on Basic instead of 17% for short stays. Long stays tend to suit apartments in residential streets, buildings that limit short stays, and owners who prefer a calmer, steadier income. Not sure which fits your apartment? Our revenue calculator gives a first estimate, and in a free consultation we look at your property and give you a realistic view of both options.

Frequently asked questions

How much does long-term property management cost in Medellín?

With Medellín Concierge, stays of 30 nights or more cost 12% of rental income plus IVA on the Basic plan, or 15% plus IVA on Premium, which also pays your property tax, HOA fees, utilities and internet. There are no upfront fees. See the full fee breakdown.

What counts as a long stay?

Any stay of 30 nights or more. Those stays are charged at the long-stay rate (12% or 15% + IVA); shorter stays are charged at the short-stay rate (17% or 20% + IVA).

Do I need an RNT for monthly rentals in Medellín?

If your apartment also takes short stays, it needs an active RNT, renewed every year, and we manage it for you. Whether stays of 30 nights or more alone need one depends on how the apartment is rented. Rules can change: this is as of November 2026, so confirm your case with a qualified professional.

Can you manage my apartment if I live outside Colombia?

Yes. We handle guests, check-ins and check-outs, cleaning, maintenance and payments, and you follow everything in your owner portal. On Premium we also pay your property tax, HOA fees, utilities and internet. Repairs above a pre-agreed limit need your approval first.

When do I get paid?

Once a month, within the first 5 business days, for the previous month. Earnings are calculated using GAAP, net of our management fees and expenses.

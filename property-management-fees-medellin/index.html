Property management fees in Medellín (2026): what owners pay

Medellín Concierge charges a commission on your rental income, plus IVA, with no upfront fees. On the Basic plan it is 12% for long stays (30+ nights) and 17% for short stays (Airbnb). On the Premium plan it is 15% and 20%, and we also pay your property taxes, HOA fees, utilities and internet and send you a monthly accounting report.

Example: what the commission looks like in pesos

Say a short-stay apartment on the Basic plan earns COP 10,000,000 in rental income in a month. The commission is 17% of that, COP 1,700,000. IVA is then added to the commission: at Colombia’s general IVA rate of 19% (Estatuto Tributario, art. 468, as of November 2026), that is COP 323,000, for a total of COP 2,023,000. The same month on a long stay would be 12%, COP 1,200,000 plus IVA.

This is an illustration of how the percentage works, not an income estimate. For a first idea of what your property could earn, try our revenue calculator. Tax rules can change: confirm how IVA and income tax apply to you with a qualified accountant.

How property management fees work in Medellín

Most managers charge in one of two ways, and some combine them:

  • A percentage of rental income. The manager earns more when you earn more and less in a slow month, so their incentive matches yours. Short stays usually cost a higher percentage than long stays because there are more check-ins, cleanings and guest messages to handle. This is how we charge.
  • A fixed monthly fee. Easier to predict, but you pay the same in a month with few bookings, and the manager earns nothing extra for filling your calendar.
  • A base fee plus a smaller percentage. A mix of the two.

A low headline percentage is not always the cheapest option. Compare what you would actually pay in a typical month, including everything below.

Extra charges to ask any manager about

  • IVA: is the quoted percentage before or after IVA? Ours is before: IVA is added.
  • Setup or onboarding fees: some managers charge to create the listing or prepare the apartment. We charge no upfront fees.
  • Cleaning: is the cleaning fee passed through at cost, or does it carry a markup?
  • Maintenance and repairs: is there a markup on contractors, and who approves the cost? With us, repairs above a limit you agree in advance need your approval first.
  • Supplies and deep cleaning: who pays for toiletries, linens and deep cleans? With us, any additional cost like these is discussed with you in advance.
  • Registrations: are RNT, SIRE and TRA included? In both our plans, they are.
  • Payouts and reporting: how often are you paid and what statements do you get? We pay the previous month’s earnings within the first 5 business days, calculated using GAAP, net of our fees and expenses.
  • Leaving: what notice is needed to end the contract, and is there a penalty? You can pause or end our services with advance notice as set out in our agreement, and we hand over smoothly.

Get the answers in writing before you sign. Our FAQ answers these for us in detail.

Fees: frequently asked questions

How much does Airbnb management cost in Medellín?

With Medellín Concierge, Airbnb (short-stay) management costs 17% of rental income plus IVA on the Basic plan, or 20% plus IVA on the Premium plan, which also pays your property taxes, HOA fees, utilities and internet. There are no upfront fees.

How much does long-term property management cost?

For stays of 30 nights or more, our commission is 12% of rental income plus IVA on the Basic plan, or 15% plus IVA on Premium.

Is IVA included in your percentages?

No. Our percentages do not include IVA (Colombian VAT). IVA is added to our commission.

Are there setup fees or hidden fees?

No. There are no upfront fees, and we operate transparently: any additional cost, such as deep cleaning, minor repairs or restocking supplies, is discussed with you in advance.

What is the difference between Basic and Premium?

Both include listing on Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia and VRBO with dynamic pricing, full guest support, cleaning and maintenance, legal compliance management and emergency support. Premium is completely hands-off: we also pay your property taxes, HOA fees, utilities and internet and send a monthly accounting report.

When and how do I get paid?

Once a month, within the first 5 business days, for the previous month. Earnings are calculated using GAAP, net of our management fees and expenses, and you can see everything in your owner portal.

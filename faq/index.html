Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how we manage your property in Medellín.

What services do you offer?

Most owners choose full management: listing optimization, guest communication, booking management, dynamic pricing, professional cleaning, maintenance coordination and 24/7 guest support. Our second option is channel management, where we give live support across the listing channels and handle guest inquiries while you keep full control of cleaning and maintenance. We also offer a brokerage agreement, where we connect you with guests and you handle the stay yourself, including communication, cleaning and maintenance.

How do you market and optimize my listing to increase bookings?

We use professional photography, compelling descriptions and strategic pricing to maximize your visibility on Airbnb. We also use SEO techniques, social media promotion and listing syndication across multiple platforms, including Booking.com, Expedia and VRBO.

Do you handle guest check-ins and check-outs?

Yes. We manage the entire guest experience, including key exchange or smart-lock access, check-in instructions and post-stay inspections to keep the property in excellent condition.

How do you screen guests to keep my property safe?

We vet guests using Airbnb’s verification system and our own screening criteria, including their ratings and communication history. We also require security deposits when necessary.

Do you provide cleaning services?

Yes. We provide professional cleaning after every guest stay. Our cleaning teams follow a detailed checklist so the property is spotless and ready for the next guest.

What happens if a guest damages my property?

We inspect the property after every stay and document any damage. If there is an issue, we handle the claim through Airbnb’s Host Guarantee and security deposit policies.

What are your management fees?

A commission on your rental income, with no upfront fees. Basic: 12% for long stays (30+ nights) and 17% for short stays. Premium: 15% and 20%, a completely hands-off service where we also pay your property taxes, HOA fees, utilities and internet bills, with a monthly accounting report. All percentages are plus IVA. Compare the plans.

Are there any additional or hidden fees?

No, we operate transparently. Any additional cost, such as deep cleaning, minor repairs or restocking supplies, is discussed with you in advance.

How much revenue can I expect to earn?

Earnings depend on location, seasonality and the type of property. We give you an estimated revenue projection based on market trends and similar listings in your area. For a first idea, try our revenue calculator.

Can I use my property for personal stays?

Of course. You keep full control of your property’s availability. Tell us the dates you want and we’ll block them for you.

How do you handle maintenance and repairs?

We work with a network of trusted vendors for regular maintenance and emergency repairs. We tell you in advance about any necessary repair and ask for your approval for costs above a pre-agreed limit.

Do you handle local short-term rental regulations?

Yes. We stay up to date on local short-term rental rules and keep your property’s registrations in order, including RNT, SIRE and TRA.

What are RNT, SIRE and TRA, and do I need them?

They are Colombian registrations required for short-term tourist rentals: the National Tourism Registry (RNT), the migration reporting system for foreign guests (SIRE), and the Tourism Registration Card (TRA). We take care of the paperwork and ongoing reporting.

How do I receive my earnings?

We pay out your earnings once a month, within the first 5 business days, for the previous month. Earnings are calculated using GAAP (Generally Accepted Accounting Principles), net of our management fees and expenses.

What if I want to pause or stop your services?

You can pause or end our services with advance notice, as set out in our agreement, and we provide a smooth handover if you decide to self-manage or switch providers.

How do you handle guest complaints and reviews?

We address guest concerns proactively to keep experiences positive. If a guest leaves a negative review, we respond professionally and work to resolve the issue.

I live outside Colombia. Can you manage everything remotely?

Yes. We handle guests, check-ins and check-outs, cleaning, maintenance, registrations and payments, and you can follow everything in your owner portal. For repairs above a pre-agreed limit, we ask for your approval first.

Do you pay agents for referrals?

Yes. Agents who refer a property owner to us receive 10% of our commission on that client, for as long as the client uses our services. Sign up as a referral agent.

How do I get started?

Contact us for a free consultation. We’ll assess your property, discuss pricing and guide you through onboarding, including creating and optimizing your listing. Request a free consultation.
